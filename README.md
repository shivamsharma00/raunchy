## Install

1. Download **Raunchy.dmg** and open it.
2. Drag **Raunchy** into **Applications**, then open him from there.
3. He appears on your screen and lives in the menu bar as 🦝 (Settings, size, sound, Open at login).
4. Optional: turn on mischief in Settings so he can walk on toolbars and steal buttons. macOS asks for Accessibility access then, not before.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later on Apple silicon. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## What he does

- **Climbs** — hops up, hooks a hand over a line of text or a toolbar, hangs, hauls himself over.
- **Steals** — grabs a button or a word, leaves a bite hole, tows it off. Click him to get it back. The real button is never touched.
- **Naps** — drapes himself over a window's edge, legs hanging, like a raccoon on a branch.
- **Likes petting** — move the cursor over him. Ignore him for a day and he sulks.
- **Water fight** — shake the mouse and the cursor becomes a water pistol. He learns your tricks.
- **Has manners** — steps away during screen sharing and full-screen apps, sits quietly on calls. `⌃⌥⌘P` sends him away and back.

## Privacy

Everything runs on your Mac. No accounts, no analytics, nothing sent anywhere. Accessibility (optional) is only used to see where buttons and text are in the front window; nothing in other apps is ever changed, and he stops looking when a password field is focused.

## Uninstall

Quit from the 🦝 menu and drag Raunchy from Applications to the Trash.
